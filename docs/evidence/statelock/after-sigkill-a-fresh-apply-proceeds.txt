A PostgreSQL advisory lock is held on a session. SIGKILL gives terraform no chance to
release anything, and the lock goes anyway because the connection goes. With state in S3
and a DynamoDB lock table the killed process leaves a row behind and the next apply is
refused until somebody force-unlocks it.
